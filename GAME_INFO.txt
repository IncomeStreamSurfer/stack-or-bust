STACK OR BUST v2.0
==================

A browser-playable PvP card battle casino game.

LIVE URL: https://incomestreamsurfer.github.io/stack-or-bust/
LOCAL: Open index.html in any browser

HOW TO PLAY:
- Login as guest or with Google (placeholder)
- Select a stake: 25¢, 50¢, $1, $2, $5, or $10
- Both players draw 3 cards
- Cards auto-reveal with damage, heals, blocks, etc.
- Player with most HP wins
- If tied, extra cards are drawn until someone wins
- Win = double your stake

FEATURES:
- Main menu with Play, Lobbies, Wallet, Friends, Profile, Ranks
- Wallet system with deposit/withdraw
- 150+ emoji avatars
- Profile with nickname and stats
- Friends list (placeholder)
- Lobbies (placeholder)
- Leaderboard (placeholder)
- Google OAuth login (placeholder)

CARDS:
- Hit: Deal 3 damage
- Block: Block next hit
- Crit: Deal 5 damage
- Heal: Restore 3 HP
- Curse: Take 2 self damage
- Drain: Deal 2, heal 2
- Pierce: Deal 2, ignores block
- Double: Deal 6 damage

CONTROLS:
- Click buttons or press Space/Enter to draw cards
- Press Space/Enter on result screen to play again

RECENT CHANGES:
- Removed multipliers (simple double or nothing)
- Added tiebreaker (extra cards if HP is equal)
